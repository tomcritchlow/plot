## FDE Sources

- [A Day in the Life of a Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineer](https://blog.palantir.com/a-day-in-the-life-of-a-palantir-forward-deployed-software-engineer-45ef2de257b1) - primary-ish Palantir description of FDSEs embedding with customers, configuring platforms, and implementing with end users.
- [So You Want to Hire a Forward Deployed Engineer](https://review.firstround.com/so-you-want-to-hire-a-forward-deployed-engineer/) - current operator view of FDE as last-mile production work, not merely solutions consulting. Useful for the AI startup context.
- [Trading Margin for Moat](https://a16z.com/services-led-growth/) - FDE as enterprise AI implementation necessity and a "trade margin for moat" business model.
- [Reflections on Palantir](https://nabeelqu.substack.com/p/reflections-on-palantir) - ex-Palantir account of FDEs going onsite, learning business processes, and feeding product development.
- [Understanding Forward Deployed Engineering](https://www.barry.ooo/posts/fde-culture) - strongest cautionary source. True FDE requires field autonomy, expensive talent, and treating deployments as R&D rather than simple COGS.
