## Working Angle

The "forward" in forward deployed engineer is not mainly geographic. It is a posture of proximity, consequence, and fieldcraft. FDE becomes interesting when read through Tom's consulting writing: ways of seeing, culture/grain, advice/usefulness, status switching, workshops as portals, and kairos timing.

## Thesis

AI is making building cheaper and faster, which means more technical people are being pushed into the field. The bottleneck shifts from "can you build it?" to "do you know where to stand, what to notice, what to build first, and how to help the organization absorb it?"

## Core Moves

- FDE is often framed as go-to-market or implementation. Reframe it as the return of the field.
- Consulting skills are not soft skills. They are the operating system for technical work inside real organizations.
- A prototype is not just a deliverable. It is a diagnostic instrument.
- Code can go with or against the organizational grain.
- Pull requests are recommendations and can be rejected by culture in the same way decks are.
- Strategy is not upstream of implementation. Implementation is one of the places strategy becomes visible.
