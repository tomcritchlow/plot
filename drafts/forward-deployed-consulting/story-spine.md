# Story spine: The Forward Part

**Premise:** Cheap building makes consulting fieldcraft more valuable—and gives the builder responsibility for what survives the prototype.

**Basis:** [Current manuscript](draft.md), [editorial checkpoint](notes.md), [source notes](sources.md). Updated 2026-09-14.

## Beats

### 1. Open the repo to discover the question

- Opening slot: a real client conversation where building changed the diagnosis. The current generalized first-person scene needs Tom's confirmation.
- [Nabeel Qureshi on Palantir](https://nabeelqu.substack.com/p/reflections-on-palantir): local knowledge informs software, then product teams generalize what works.

### 2. Forward means staying close to consequences

- Culture appears in workarounds, permissions, language and the supposedly temporary spreadsheet. [Ways of Seeing](https://tomcritchlow.com/2018/10/29/ways-of-seeing/)
- Proximity matters when the respectable requirement turns out to differ from the live problem.

**Keep (draft):** “Forward is proximity plus consequence.”

### 3. The prototype becomes a diagnostic instrument

- A small working thing elicits corrections, reveals dependencies and exposes the organization's assumptions.
- Extend [Workshops as Portals](https://tomcritchlow.com/2019/09/23/workshops/): the artifact creates permission to encounter reality.

**Keep (draft):** “A prototype is a question with a runtime.”

### 4. Working code still has to enter a culture

- A pull request is also a recommendation. New ownership, budgets and habits can determine whether it is used.
- [The Consultant's Grain](https://tomcritchlow.com/2017/07/18/the-consultants-grain/): learn when to amplify existing intent, challenge it, or stop building.

### 5. Build a mirror the organization can use

- [Don't Give Advice, Be Useful](https://tomcritchlow.com/2024/01/23/advice/): help the client see the problem before prescribing its resources.
- [Status switching](https://tomcritchlow.com/2020/06/24/navigating-power-status/) and [kairos](https://tomcritchlow.com/2021/01/26/kairos/) explain how to introduce the artifact, who can act, and when the intervention can land.

### 6. The prototype needs an exit

- Hypothetical reconciliation script reveals incompatible definitions of “customer.” Permanent glue could hide the disagreement again.
- Name the learning objective and what follows: retire it, change the source system, or assign a production owner. Dependencies outlive the consultant.

### 7. Implementation is where strategy encounters resistance

- The API refuses; permissions are missing; the user ignores the button. Each response tests the proposed strategy.
- Building faster increases the value of choosing the right intervention and helping the organization absorb it.

## Landing

- Return to the opening: the client answers by using, correcting, resisting or ignoring the thing.

**Keep (draft):** “The forward part is staying for the answer.”
