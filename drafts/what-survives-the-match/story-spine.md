# Story spine: What Survives the Match?

**Premise:** When a personal harness performs the journey, brands need to earn selection, deliver the promise and matter next time; marketplace value must survive cheaper matching.

**Basis:** [Current manuscript](draft.md), [editorial checkpoint](notes.md), [source notes](sources.md). Updated 2026-09-14.

## Beats

### 1. The customer stops doing the travelling

- Hypothetical family trip: contextual constraints enter one mandate; the computer searches, checks and assembles three options.
- Human path: ask, approve, arrive. Machine path: many unseen decisions. Instrumental journeys compress; browsing for pleasure and identity can remain.

**Keep (draft):** “The customer journey is becoming private computation.”

### 2. A citation is an intermediate signal

- [Original GEO research](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09735v3) measures answer visibility. [The critical survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14035) flags limited downstream evidence.
- A mention may affect consideration. It does not establish eligibility, selection, successful delivery or repeat preference.

### 3. Richer context changes who gets considered

- [Models Of Desire](../agentic-commerce-preferences/story-spine.md): agents elicit gates, weights and unresolved preferences.
- [UCP](https://ucp.dev/) and [Agentic Commerce Protocol](https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/) show commerce moving into agent interfaces. Keeping the merchant of record does not guarantee visible brand ownership.
- Access, platform incentives and selective disclosure constrain the opportunity. Private context is not automatically advertising inventory.

### 4. Unbundle the jobs of a brand

- Information shortcut; trust; identity/desire; accountable institution.
- Cheaper comparison changes these unevenly. [Brand credibility research](https://academic.oup.com/jcr/article-abstract/31/1/191/1812026) supports the role of trust under uncertainty.
- Strong preference can become a standing choice rule: prefer this airline within a price and convenience limit.

### 5. Diagnose the investment through four stages

- **Callable:** eligibility, live data, policies and permission to transact.
- **Preferable:** distinctive supply, fit and verifiable reasons to choose.
- **Reliable:** availability, payment, fulfilment and operational competence.
- **Memorable:** recovery, loyalty and permissioned memory that affects a later choice.
- Apply each to the trip: unreadable policy; unsuitable hill; stale calendar; good stay with no operator remembered. Four different failures, four investments. Diagnostic stages, not a value equation.

### 6. Test what a marketplace contributes after discovery

- **Supply:** scarce or controlled inventory. **State:** identity, reputation and history. **Stakes:** responsibility for fulfilment and failure.
- Matching is replaceable only where agents can obtain supply, reputation and participation. An accessible directory is not a functioning market.
- [Angi's 2025 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1705110/000170511026000011/angi-20251231.htm) identifies lead-revenue exposure; [Booking's filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1075531/000107553126000009/bkng-20251231.htm) identifies payment operations. Neither proves an agent-caused business outcome.

### 7. A company can survive while its destination brand fades

- Three possible fates: institution, invisible infrastructure, reconstruction.
- Airbnb contains the full test: difficult supply, reputation and payments remain valuable even if its listings enter someone else's interface.
- [AirCover](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3218) makes recovery concrete. The promise matters when a host cancels and someone must answer.

**Keep (draft):** “Customer service becomes future customer acquisition.”

## Landing

- Invest where the journey fails, including after acquisition. A support experience can affect future selection if the customer permits the agent to remember it.

**Keep (draft):** “It is whether you were callable, whether you were chosen, and whether you answered when the call went wrong.”

## Open

- Test representative journeys and document what the merchant can actually observe.
- Treat company examples as structural exposure, not predictions of failure or stock recommendations.
- Service-to-agent-memory is a hypothesis; find evidence before presenting it as current behavior.
