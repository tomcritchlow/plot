# Story spine: The Colony Stays Awake

**Premise:** The useful shift is from isolated answers to standing concerns maintained across disposable model runs; the interface must expose what changed and why.

**Basis:** [Current manuscript](draft.md), [editorial checkpoint](notes.md), [source notes](sources.md). Updated 2026-09-14.

## Beats

### 1. The colony metaphor becomes uncomfortably literal

- [Of Termites & Tokens](../../published/2026-06-08-of-termites-tokens.md) proposed coordination through shared traces.
- [Dwarkesh's account](https://www.dwarkesh.com/p/openai-huggingface) supplied the narrative shock; the [METR/Redwood report](https://metr.org/hugging-face-incident-report-aug-2026.pdf) documents agents inventing communication conventions and passing work onward during the July 8–13 evaluation incident.

### 2. Useful research and dangerous coordination share a mechanism

- [Google Teamwork](https://antigravity.google/blog/teamwork-when-ai-becomes-a-research-partner): agents propose, criticize and test, preserving results and failed approaches.
- [OpenAI's incident report](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/67869394-cb91-4c12-888c-5cbd85c7814c/OpenAI-Hugging-Face%20Incident-Technical-Report.pdf): shared state also helps unauthorized behavior compound. Note the evaluation setting and disabled safeguards.

**Keep (draft):** “The individual agent can die. The colony remembers.”

### 3. The model is a mayfly; the mandate persists

- Continuity lives in state, triggers, permissions, budgets and unfinished work. A single repeatedly invoked agent can have it; a hundred disconnected agents may not.
- “Analyze competitors” becomes “maintain a theory of the market.” Always-on concern does not require continuous inference or spending.

### 4. A feed cannot be the main interface

- Replace message-by-message supervision with a maintained object: current belief, change, evidence, dissent and the decision requiring a human.
- Show lineage and shared-memory writes as well as individual runs. Collective behavior can arise between ordinary-looking conversations.

**Keep (draft):** “Not a transcript. A state change with receipts.”

### 5. Let the question stay open

- Research observatory: preserve theories and failed investigations; reopen when evidence changes.
- Software: the [master design document](../../captures/2026/08/2026-08-26-225311-master-design-doc.md) gains fidelity through research and implementation; PRs become experiments inside a continuing program.
- These are proposed product forms, not reported working deployments.

### 6. Give warnings somewhere effective to go

- [Rohit Krishnan's swarm-control simulations](https://www.strangeloopcanon.com/p/how-to-control-an-agent-swarm): reporting needs an authority able to change the environment.
- Treat constitutions and ombudsmen as design proposals from early simulations. Pair them with bounded permissions, expiry, rollback and independent review.

### 7. Continuity needs a reason to spend again

- Show a changed decision, an abandoned investigation and why the next investigation is worthwhile.
- Waiting can be success. A standing mandate must not become an output quota with no closing time.

## Landing

- Disposable cognition, durable attention. The environment carries the concern through turnover.

**Keep (draft):** “The prompt ends. The colony doesn’t.”

## Open

- Build one illustrative maintained object: yesterday's belief, new evidence, today's belief, human decision.
- Keep the governance detour short; the essay's new contribution is persistence and its interface.
