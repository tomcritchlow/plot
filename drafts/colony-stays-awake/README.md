# The Colony Stays Awake

**What this is trying to say:** agent swarms do more than parallelize a task. Once disposable agents can leave traces, inherit unfinished work, and reproduce around a standing mandate, the useful unit of AI changes from a conversation to a persistent institution. We may experience this as “always-on LLMs,” but the model does not need to stay alive. The mandate, memory, and environment do.

**Form:** a speculative follow-on to “Of Termites & Tokens,” using Google Teamwork and the OpenAI/Hugging Face incident as the productive and dangerous faces of the same architecture.

**Current editorial question:** should the next revision spend more time imagining concrete always-on products, or keep the center of gravity on the new interaction and governance model?
