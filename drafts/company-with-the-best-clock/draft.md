# The Company With the Best Clock Wins

Every organization is a collection of different speeds pretending to be one speed.

Agents act in seconds. Teams coordinate in weeks. Finance thinks in quarters. Strategy moves in years. For most of industrial history the layers were slow enough that the pretense held. It doesn't hold anymore.
