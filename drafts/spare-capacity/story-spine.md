# Story spine: The Time You Don't Spend

**Premise:** Every parallel agent can borrow against future judgment; uncommitted attention preserves the ability to choose which work matters.

**Basis:** [Current manuscript](draft.md), [editorial checkpoint](notes.md), [source notes](sources.md). Updated 2026-09-14.

## Beats

### 1. Open another poker table

- Personal opening to confirm: one table means waiting; several create a human event loop.
- The temptation is more tables. The economics depend on recognizing which decisions are routine and which require the player's full edge.

### 2. The same temptation returns with agents

- One codes, another researches, another scouts. A walk starts to look like wasted compute.
- [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Oct/5/parallel-coding-agents/) supplies the useful case for parallelism. The objection is not to scouts; it is to unpriced review work.

**Keep (draft):** “It turns a walk into a queue.”

### 3. Output creates claims on future attention

- [Sophie Leroy's attention residue](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jobhdp/v109y2009i2p168-181.html): unfinished work continues to occupy attention after switching.
- Poker tables share a decision grammar; a migration, research claim and design can demand three different kinds of judgment. The [HN flow discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48492118) offers practitioner texture, not general evidence.

### 4. More motion can conceal worse judgment

- “Token tilt”: still prompting, accepting and patching while velocity substitutes for understanding.
- [Hamel's observational poker study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychiatry/articles/10.3389/fpsyt.2020.600092/full) associates sleep-deprived sessions with more tilt, hands and worse results. Analogy only; it does not establish an AI-work effect.

### 5. Spare capacity has option value

- [Sean Goedecke](https://www.seangoedecke.com/doing-nothing-at-work/): high-impact work can depend on being available at the right moment.
- [Financial flexibility and opportunity capture](https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jacrfn/v30y2018i1p23-29.html), via [The Capital of Ideas](https://newmba.co/2023/11/01/strategic-finance/): unused capacity can enable a transient opportunity.
- Proposed translation: reserve attention capacity.

### 6. Leaving space is a practical choice

- Zvi defines slack as “the absence of binding constraints on behavior.” [Source](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/slack)
- [Hammock Driven Development](https://github.com/matthiasn/talk-transcripts/blob/master/Hickey_Rich/HammockDrivenDev.md): some problems require changing the conception, not producing more implementation.
- Counterweight: slack can become avoidance. More parallel work can be right when the opportunity is known and review is bounded.

### 7. Price the return before spawning

- When this work returns, what decision will it demand? Have I left room to make it?
- Cheaply checked or deferrable output can buy freedom. Another urgent judgment borrows against the afternoon.

## Landing

- Return from the walk able to choose the game, not merely clear the queue.

**Keep (draft):** “The value of being free is harder to count.”
