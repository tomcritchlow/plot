## Working Thesis

Agentic commerce is not just better shopping automation. It is a new preference infrastructure.

The old web inferred preferences from visible behavior: clicks, searches, carts, purchases, loyalty records, reviews, returns. Agentic commerce can elicit and operationalize a thicker model: constraints, substitutions, values, aversions, unresolved intentions, bargaining posture, willingness to pay, and the difference between hard gates and soft weights.

The provocative turn:

> Agentic commerce does not merely give consumers better agents. It gives markets better-described consumers.

## Core Tensions

- Better search can increase efficiency and prices at the same time.
- Preferences are not always pre-existing objects waiting to be found.
- Agents may resolve choice conflict, turning deferred demand into transactions.
- Preference models are useful for consumers and valuable against consumers.
- The real frontier may be preference rights, not shopping convenience.

## Useful Handles

### The Preference Stack

Preferences have layers:

- Attributes: red, hardback, nearby, under $40, available tomorrow.
- Consequences: saves me a trip, avoids Amazon, feels durable, solves the actual situation.
- Values: independence, care, thrift, taste, environmental concern, loyalty to a certain kind of commerce.

The agent has to know which layer it is operating on.

### Gates And Weights

Some preferences are negotiable weights. Others are hard gates.

The interesting thing is that surface language does not reveal which is which. "It has to be red" might be literal, or might mean visible, sentimental, easy to recognize, or gift-like. "I prefer independent stores" might be a soft preference, a moral constraint, a trust heuristic, or a willingness-to-pay signal.

### Deferred Demand

A lot of non-consumption is not lack of desire. It is unresolved conflict.

Examples:

- The trip not booked.
- The gift not bought.
- The subscription not cancelled.
- The card rate not renegotiated.
- The shoes not replaced.
- The restaurant not chosen.

Agents can monetize all the "I should probably..." moments that never become transactions.

### Preference Wallet

Possible product handle:

> A preference wallet is a place where tastes, constraints, substitutions, unresolved intentions, and bargaining posture live under user control.

This could become a stronger late-section idea. It connects Doc Searls / VRM, preference privacy, portability, selective disclosure, and consumer-side bargaining power.
