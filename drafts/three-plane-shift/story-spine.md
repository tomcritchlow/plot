# Story spine: An Almanac for the Age of Chaos

**Premise:** When customers, channels and craft change together, marketing needs ways to orient people and coordinate action through uncertainty.

**Basis:** Retrospective spine of the [published July 2026 essay](../../published/2026-07-30-an-almanac-for-the-age-of-chaos.md), also [on Alephic](https://www.alephic.com/writing/almanac-for-the-age-of-chaos). Added 2026-09-14 for navigation; this is not a new manuscript revision. [Draft history](README.md) · [Source notes](sources.md).

## Beats

### 1. Place the reader in Paris, 1889

- Eiffel Tower, Poincaré and Michelin: engineering confidence, the limits of prediction and a company helping people navigate a changing world.
- The three-body problem frames the essay's felt instability. Treat it as an analogy, not proof that business forecasting is impossible.

### 2. The customer gains a cognitive sidecar

- The published Vancouver/starfish anecdote shows recommendations shaped by family context and practical constraints.
- Delegation changes who researches and compares before the customer reaches a brand. [Matt Webb's UbiGPT](https://interconnected.org/home/2023/10/06/ubigpt) supplies related framing.

### 3. The channels change around that customer

- Search becomes generated interpretation; content access and licensing disrupt its old bargain.
- Social faces synthetic abundance, spam and new creative possibilities. The essay's [publisher blocking study](https://www.buzzstream.com/blog/publishers-block-ai-study/) is dated evidence, not a current market estimate.

### 4. The craft changes inside the team

- Agents expand production and experimentation while increasing the volume humans must inspect.
- Customer behavior, distribution and production pull on one another: automating the inherited workflow may accelerate yesterday's marketing.

**Keep (published):** “The two bottlenecks are now imagination and coordination.”

### 5. Faster production requires better coordination traces

- [Coase's firm, via Matt Webb](https://interconnected.org/home/2014/12/23/corporations): coordination has costs.
- Extend [Of Termites & Tokens](../../published/2026-06-08-of-termites-tokens.md): maintained context, provenance, permissions and status let people and agents work from shared conditions.

**Keep (published):** “Railroads needed standard time. Maybe agentic companies need standard status.”

### 6. Customers need orientation too

- [Michelin's history](https://guide.michelin.com/th/en/history-of-the-michelin-guide-th): help motorists travel, thereby expanding what the product makes possible.
- Proposed modern almanacs: useful guidance for shopping, interiors or meal planning, grounded in what AI actually can and cannot do.
- Brand-specific evaluations should precede the guide. These are proposals in the essay, not claimed deployments.

### 7. Return to electricity and distinguish spectacle from use

- Illuminated fountains versus the promises of [electro-mania](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/8717/pg8717.html.utf8).
- [Nikhil Suresh's AI-mania critique](https://hermit-tech.com/blog/ai-mania-is-eviscerating-global-decisionmaking) supplies the modern foil: uncertainty still permits considered action.

## Landing

**Keep (published):** “The answer to a world that can no longer be predicted is not better prediction. It is better orientation.”
