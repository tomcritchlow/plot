I've been noticing that the organizations I admire don't move fast so much as they move *in time* — decisions land on a beat, reviews happen on a beat, and everyone can predict when the next beat falls.

The organizations that struggle aren't slow. They're arrhythmic. Nobody knows when a decision will arrive, so everyone hedges, and hedging is expensive.

I don't have a full theory of this yet, but I suspect tempo is closer to the root of organizational health than speed is.
