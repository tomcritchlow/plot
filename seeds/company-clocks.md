# Companies Need Clocks

Organizations increasingly contain actors operating at radically different speeds.

Agents operate in seconds.
Teams coordinate weekly.
Finance plans quarterly.
Strategy might change annually.

Perhaps coordination infrastructure increasingly becomes temporal infrastructure.

## Questions

- What does an organizational clock actually consist of?
- Is "standard status" analogous to standard time?
- What changes once agents operate continuously?
