What if, instead of the loop of: write brief → AI writes PRs → review PRs → repeat, we work from a master design document?

The master design document is a design of the whole system and evolves in complexity as we learn things. Sometimes we learn things from building; sometimes we learn things from research. Over time, we add fidelity to the design document, and that's how we steer PRs...?
