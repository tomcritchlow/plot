> In short, we will transition from an era of *proof scarcity* to an era of *proof abundance*. Most of our institutions — journals, priority conventions, hiring and promotion criteria, prizes, the very notion of a research program — were designed under the assumption of scarcity, and it should not surprise us if they behave poorly under abundance.

## Note

From scarcity → abundance. Feels like we'll see the same thing for more than proofs, but code and all knowledge work... good analogy.
